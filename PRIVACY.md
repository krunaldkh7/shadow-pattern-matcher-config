# Privacy Policy — Shadow Pattern Matcher

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Shadow Pattern Matcher ("the app", "we") is a puzzle game made by Krunal Dudhagara.
This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Contact: **krunaldudhagara7777@gmail.com**

## What stays on your device

Your game progress (solved levels, bulbs, best times, points), the levels you
draw yourself, and your settings are stored only on your device. They are not
sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

## What is collected, and by whom

### Advertising — Google AdMob
The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may
collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on
Android), IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

- **iPhone:** the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency).
  If you choose **Ask App Not to Track**, the advertising identifier is not
  shared and you see non-personalised ads.
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad
  personalisation, in your device's Google settings.

Google's policy: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

### Usage analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase
To understand how the game is played and improve it, the app sends usage events
to Google Analytics for Firebase: screens opened, levels started, solved or
skipped, hints used, and similar game events, together with an app instance ID,
device model, operating system, app version, and approximate country (from your
IP address). We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise
location.

Google's policy: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>

### Global leaderboard — Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore
If you open the leaderboard and choose to join it, the app stores:

- an anonymous user ID created by Firebase Authentication (no email, phone or
  password), and
- the **display name you choose** and your **total points**.

Your display name and points are **visible to all players** on the leaderboard.
Please don't use your real name or personal information as your display name.

### Ad settings file
When it starts, the app downloads a small settings file from GitHub to decide
which ads to show. GitHub receives your IP address as part of that request.
GitHub's policy: <https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies>

## How the information is used

- to show ads and keep the game free;
- to understand which levels and features players use, and fix problems;
- to show the global leaderboard.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them. If you believe a child has joined the
leaderboard, contact us and we will remove the entry.

## Your choices and deleting your data

- **Leaderboard:** to remove your leaderboard entry, email us the display name
  you used. We will delete it.
- **Progress and drawn levels:** Settings → Reset progress, or delete the app.
- **Ads and tracking:** see the iPhone and Android options above.
- **Analytics:** analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months and is not
  linked to your name or contact details.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
