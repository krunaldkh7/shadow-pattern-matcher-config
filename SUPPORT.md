## Common questions

**How do I play?**
Drag with one finger to turn the gold wire; twist with two fingers to spin it.
When its shadow lines up with the dotted outline, the level is solved.
In the app: Home → How to play.

**What are the bulbs?**
Each level has 3 bulbs. The top bulb button turns the wire halfway to the answer
and uses one bulb; the bulbs you keep are your stars. The eye button shows a
ghost of the answer, 3 times per level. When either runs out, you can watch an
ad for another hint.

**Can I skip a level?**
Yes: tap Skip and watch a short video. The next level opens, and you can come
back to solve the skipped one later.

**How are points counted?**
100 for solving a level, +50 for each bulb kept, and up to +60 for solving it in
under a minute. Only your best result on each level counts. Skipped levels and
levels you draw yourself don't earn points.

**How do I change or remove my leaderboard name?**
Email us the name you used and we'll change or remove it.

**I lost my progress.**
Progress is saved on your phone only. Deleting the app, or Settings → Reset
progress, clears it.

**I don't want personalised ads.**
On iPhone, choose "Ask App Not to Track" (or Settings → Privacy & Security →
Tracking). On Android, use your device's Google → Ads settings.
